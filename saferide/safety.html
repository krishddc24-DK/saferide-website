Safety

Safety built into every trip

These features are part of the SafeRide app. Here's how each one works.

Join the waitlist

In an emergency, call 111.

Before you ride

Checked documents

Every driver uploads 8 documents, including their licence, P (passenger) endorsement, insurance, certificate of fitness and vehicle registration.

Approved by our team

Drivers can't go online until an admin approves them. An expired document blocks them from going online.

At pickup

Your PIN starts the trip

The trip can't start until your driver enters the 4-digit PIN you give them.

Check the vehicle

If the wrong vehicle type turns up and you cancel, you pay $0.

Time to get there

Your driver waits up to 5 minutes, and you'll hear when there are 2 minutes left.

During your trip

Live tracking

Follow your trip on the map.

SOS button

Tap SOS, confirm, and the app dials 111.

In-app calls and chat

Talk to your driver in the app. Calls don't dial a phone number.

Rested drivers

  • Drivers are limited to 7 hours of continuous work, then a 30-minute break
  • 13 hours a day, then 10 hours' rest
  • 70 hours in total, then 24 hours' rest
  • An “Are you OK?” check after 20 minutes without activity; offline at 30

These follow NZTA work-time rules, checked in the app and on our servers.

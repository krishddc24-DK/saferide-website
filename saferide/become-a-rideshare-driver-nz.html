How to become a rideshare driver in New Zealand

What you need, the documents to prepare, and the work-time rules to know.

Apply to drive

  2. Documents

What you need

To carry paying passengers in New Zealand you need a P (passenger) endorsement on your driver licence. You'll also need a vehicle with a current certificate of fitness, registration and insurance, and the right to work in New Zealand.

Documents to prepare

  • Profile photo
  • Driver licence, front and back
  • P (passenger) endorsement
  • Insurance
  • Transport service licence
  • Certificate of fitness
  • Vehicle registration

These are the 8 documents SafeRide asks for. Other platforms may ask for different ones.

Launch

When does SafeRide launch?

We're launching in Wellington first. We haven't set a public date yet, so join the waitlist and we'll email you when the app is ready.

Which cities will SafeRide cover?

Wellington first. Auckland, Christchurch and Hamilton are on our list. Join the waitlist for your city so we know where demand is.

Is the app on the App Store and Google Play?

Not yet. It's coming to iPhone and Android. The store buttons on this site will take you there once it's live.

Booking

Can I book a ride for later?

Yes. You can book for today or up to 3 days ahead, in 15-minute steps, at least 20 minutes from now. The ride is sent to drivers 20 minutes before pickup.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Add their name and mobile and the driver will see them. In-app calls come to you, because the passenger doesn't have the app.

What ride types are there?

Small Car, Medium, SUV and Van (10 seater). A larger vehicle can pick up a smaller ride type.

Can I add stops or a note for my driver?

Yes. Add stops along the way and a note such as “Door 3, arrivals” when you book.

Payments

How is my fare worked out?

$3.00 to start, plus $1.70 per km and 40c per minute along the driving route with live traffic, with a $5.00 minimum. That's adjusted for your ride type, then a $3.00 booking fee is added. You see the total before you book.

When is my card charged?

When you book, the fare is held on your card. It's charged when your driver starts the trip with your PIN.

Is there surge pricing?

No. SafeRide doesn't currently use surge pricing, so the same rates apply at busy times.

What if I cancel?

Cancel before a driver accepts and you pay $0. After a driver accepts, there's a $5.00 cancellation fee. See all the cases on our Pricing page.

How can I pay?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay on a secure Stripe payment page.

Is there an airport fee?

Yes, $6.00 when a trip starts or ends within 2 km of Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch or Hamilton airport. It's included in the price you see, and it goes to your driver.

Safety

What is the pickup PIN?

A 4-digit code you see in the app once a driver accepts. Tell it to your driver; the trip can't start without it.

How are drivers checked?

Drivers upload 8 documents, including their licence, P endorsement and insurance, and an admin approves them before they can go online.

What if I feel unsafe during a trip?

Use the SOS button in the app. It asks you to confirm, then dials 111.

Can my driver see my phone number?

Calls in the app don't dial a phone number. Your booking details are shared with your driver so they can complete the trip.

Drivers

What does SafeRide charge drivers?

A flat 10% of the trip fare. You keep airport, cancellation and no-show fees in full.

When do drivers get paid?

Pay weeks run Monday to Sunday 11:59 pm and are paid the following Monday.

What do I need to drive?

A P (passenger) endorsement, a full licence, and documents including insurance, a certificate of fitness and vehicle registration. See the full list on Drive with SafeRide.

Are there limits on how long I can drive?

Yes. The app follows NZTA work-time rules: 7 hours continuous, 13 hours a day and 70 hours in total before you need a break.

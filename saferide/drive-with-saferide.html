Drive with SafeRide · Wellington

Drive in Wellington and keep 90% of every fare

A flat 10% fee, one job offer at a time, and airport, cancellation and no-show fees paid to you in full.

Apply to driveSee requirements

Know what you'll earn

The SafeRide fee is a flat 10% of the trip fare. Everything else in the table is yours.

Your earnings screen shows your current pay week and next payout.
ItemYou get
Trip fare90%
Airport fee ($6.00)100%
Cancellation fee ($5.00)100%
No-show fee ($5.00)100%
Pay weekMonday to Sunday 11:59 pm, paid the following Monday

Fair job offers

One at a time

Each job goes to one driver at a time, closest first, then whoever has waited longest. No racing to tap.

15 seconds to decide

Your phone rings and says “You have a new job, from SafeRide”, even in the background.

Cover Jobs

Rides nobody has taken after 2 minutes appear as optional Cover Jobs for drivers within 30 km. They don't ring.

What you'll need

Documents to upload

  • Profile photo
  • Driver licence (front and back)
  • P (passenger) endorsement
  • Insurance
  • Transport service licence
  • Certificate of fitness
  • Vehicle registration

Details we'll ask for in the app

  • Licence version
  • Date of birth
  • Home address
  • Right to work in NZ (and visa if needed)
  • IRD and GST details
  • Bank account
  • Insurer

You'll also accept the Driver Terms and Contract. Read them on our Driver Terms page.

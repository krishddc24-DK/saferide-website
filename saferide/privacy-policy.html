1. Who we are

Saferide Co operates the SafeRide app and this website. We're based in Wellington, New Zealand. We follow the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect on this website

What you type into our forms (for example your email, name, city, and for drivers your mobile number, P endorsement status and vehicle type). If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects information about how you use the site, such as pages visited and device type.

3. What we collect in the app

Riders: name, email, mobile, home city, emergency contact, trip details including pickup and drop-off, location during trips, ratings and messages.

Drivers: the identity, licence, vehicle, tax, bank and compliance details needed to approve and pay drivers, plus location while online and work-time records.

Card details are entered on Stripe's payment page; we don't see or store full card numbers.

4. How we use it

To run the waitlist and tell you about the launch, to respond to support and contact requests, to provide trips, payments and safety features, to approve and pay drivers, to meet NZTA work-time rules, and to improve the service.

5. Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run SafeRide, including Stripe (payments), Mapbox (maps and routes), Supabase (data hosting), Expo and Firebase (notifications), Stream (in-app calls) and Google Analytics (website analytics, only with consent). Your driver or rider sees what's needed for the trip. We share information with authorities where the law requires. We don't sell personal information.

Placeholder Website form provider to be added once connected.

6. How long we keep it

Waitlist details until launch communications end or you unsubscribe. When an account is deleted, it's closed 7 days after the deletion is scheduled and erased 23 days after that. Some driver records are kept longer where the law requires, including tax records for 7 years and work-time records.

7. Your rights

You can ask to see or correct your personal information, and you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner if you're unhappy with how we've handled it.

8. Cookies

We only use analytics cookies if you accept them. You can change your choice any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

10. Changes

We'll update this page and the date above if this policy changes.

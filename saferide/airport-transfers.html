How airport pricing works

Trips that start or end within 2 km of these airports include a $6.00 airport fee. It's part of the price you see, and it goes to your driver in full.

Booking your airport ride

  1. Pick a day

    Today or up to 3 days ahead.

  2. Pick a time

    In 15-minute steps, at least 20 minutes from now.

  3. Pay

    The fare is held on your card.

  4. Ride goes out

    Your ride goes to drivers 20 minutes before pickup.

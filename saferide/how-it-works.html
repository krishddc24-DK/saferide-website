How it works

From booking to drop-off, with no surprises

Here's exactly what happens when you book a SafeRide, and when you pay.

Join the waitlist

Your trip, step by step

  1. Set pickup and drop-off

    Search any New Zealand address or place. Add stops if you need them.

  2. Check your price

    Choose a ride type and see the fare, worked out from the driving route with live traffic.

  3. Pay securely

    Pay on the Stripe page by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The amount is held, not charged.

  4. A driver accepts

    You'll see who's coming and follow them on the map. Your 4-digit pickup PIN appears.

  5. Share your PIN

    Tell your driver the PIN. The trip starts only when they enter it, and that's when you're charged.

  6. Arrive and rate

    Rate your trip and download a PDF receipt from your ride history.

Choose the right ride

Small Car

For everyday trips on your own or with a friend.

Medium

A bit more room for passengers and bags.

SUV

Extra space for luggage and groups.

Van (10 seater)

For groups of up to 10. Van jobs go to van drivers.

A larger vehicle can pick up a smaller ride type.

Make it work for you

Stops along the way

Add stops when you book.

Note for your driver

Add details like “Door 3, arrivals”.

Book for someone else

Enter their name and mobile so the driver can find them. In-app calls still come to you, since they won't have the app.

Stay in the loop

Follow your driver live on the map and chat in the app. Calls happen in the app, so no phone number is dialled. You'll also hear spoken alerts when your driver is on the way, when they arrive, and when you have 2 minutes of waiting time left.

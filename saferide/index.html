For riders

Why ride with SafeRide

Straightforward pricing and a pickup you can trust.

See the price first

Your fare comes from the actual driving route with live traffic. You see it before you book.

Pay when the trip starts

We hold the fare on your card when you book. It's only charged when your driver starts the trip with your PIN.

$0 if plans change

Cancel before a driver accepts and you pay nothing. If no driver is found in 10 minutes, the hold is released.

Book ahead

Schedule a ride for today or up to 3 days ahead, in 15-minute steps.

Four steps from booking to drop-off

  1. Set your trip

    Choose pickup, drop-off and ride type. See your price.

  2. Confirm and pay

    Pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The fare is held, not taken.

  3. Meet your driver

    Follow them on the map. You'll get a 4-digit pickup PIN.

  4. Share your PIN

    Your driver enters it to start the trip. That's when you're charged.

For drivers

Keep 90% of every fare

SafeRide takes a flat 10% fee. Airport, cancellation and no-show fees go to you in full. Jobs are offered to one driver at a time, so you're not racing anyone for them.

Quick answers

When does SafeRide launch?

We're launching in Wellington first. We haven't set a public date yet, so join the waitlist and we'll email you when the app is ready.

How is my fare worked out?

$3.00 to start, plus $1.70 per km and 40c per minute along the driving route with live traffic, with a $5.00 minimum. That's adjusted for your ride type, then a $3.00 booking fee is added. You see the total before you book.

When is my card charged?

When you book, the fare is held on your card. It's charged when your driver starts the trip with your PIN.

Is there surge pricing?

No. SafeRide doesn't currently use surge pricing, so the same rates apply at busy times.
